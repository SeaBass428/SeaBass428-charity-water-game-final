# Charity: water Game Final

The soundtrack list shows the music used on the website. Use **Play** to start the background loop, the progress bar or skip buttons to move through the song, and **Mute** to silence it. The game does not autoplay music.

Sound effects are enabled by default and can be turned off separately. Pipe rotations, flowing water, leaks, and victories each have their own effect; sounds begin after your first game interaction.

Choose **Normal** or **Hard** from the game mode menu. Hard mode gives you less time and a more winding route. Changing modes starts a new game and resets the score and level.

The campaign has 10 levels. The progress tracker shows your completed levels and upcoming milestones: earn 250 bonus points at level 3, 500 at level 5, and 1,000 at level 10. Each milestone bonus can only be earned once per campaign, and completing level 10 finishes the game.
